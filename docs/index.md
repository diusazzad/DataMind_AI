# Welcome to DataMind AI

**DataMind AI** is an enterprise-grade Intelligent Data & Document Assistant.

Our platform empowers users to:

1. **Analyze Data:** Instantly process and visualize CSV/Excel data.
2. **Chat with Databases:** Use natural language to generate SQL and query PostgreSQL (Text-to-SQL).
3. **Document Intelligence (RAG):** Chat with your PDFs and get accurate answers backed by source citations.

## Why DataMind AI?
Bridging the gap between complex databases, unstructured documents, and end-users through natural language processing.
